Phuzzbox

Effects pedals for bass guitar and other line inputs, computed live from the physics of their circuits and mechanisms: transistors, tubes, transformers, loudspeakers, springs and tape. No samples, no captured profiles. Plug an audio interface into the browser, or play the test bass, a bass made of physics, from your computer keys. A sibling of Forte.

Every page plays the test bass, a bass made of physics, and has a tuner that reads a stiff string's first partial.
